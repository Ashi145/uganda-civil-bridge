About UG Civil Bridge

A clearer starting point for public-service concerns.

A citizen-focused bridge between everyday problems and the organisations responsible for public services — designed around clarity, careful handling and a record citizens can return to.

Independent platformNo account requiredNo public complaints feed
The starting point

Across communities, citizens experience problems with public services.

Sometimes they know exactly what is wrong, but they are unsure where to report it, which organisation is responsible or how to follow up. A pothole, a leaking pipe or a damaged public facility can remain unreported simply because the starting point is unclear.

UG Civil Bridge provides a centralised starting point for reporting selected public-service concerns, directing submissions through available official communication channels and giving citizens a reference number.

Our purpose

To make it easier for citizens to report selected public-service concerns clearly, responsibly and with a reference they can follow. We want the first question — "Where do I report this?" — to have an understandable answer.

"One clear place to start, a structured way to report, and a reference to follow up."

The problem we address

Citizens may not know which organisation handles which service. Reporting channels can be difficult to find or understand. Without a structured record, it is hard to know what was submitted, when, and to whom.

This platform addresses the starting point — not the underlying service delivery, which remains the responsibility of the receiving organisation.

Our role and limitations

UG Civil Bridge is an independent citizen-reporting platform. It helps organise and forward complaints through available official communication channels. It is not a government agency and does not claim to represent, control or operate the organisations listed.

  • We do not guarantee acknowledgement or resolution.
  • We do not claim a complaint was read by an official unless that is recorded.
  • Forwarding uses only approved organisation destinations.

Responsible information handling

Every complaint is treated as potentially sensitive. We design around data minimisation: collecting what is needed to describe and route the concern, limiting who can access records, and avoiding any public display of complaint details or contact information.

  • Contact details are not shown publicly.
  • Tracking requires contact verification, not just a reference.
  • Production access must use server-side authentication and audit logs.

Four steps. One clear record.

Here is what happens from the moment you decide to report a concern, and what the platform can — and cannot — do at each stage.

1
Step one

Identify

Review the organisation directory. Choose KCCA, NWSC or EPSCF based on the concern. Relevant categories only appear after selection.

2
Step two

Submit

Complete the guided form with a title, description, location, contact information and an optional photograph. The form validates details before accepting the report.

3
Step three

A mock reference is generated in this prototype. In production, the backend would issue a unique number and store the record centrally after validation.

4
Step four

Follow up

Return with your reference and contact verification. See what was recorded, when it was routed and whether any acknowledgement or response is available.

How routing is intended to work

The organisation directory supplies an approved destination. Production would use a server-side service to select that destination, send a professionally formatted complaint through an authorised email provider, and record every delivery attempt.

KCCA and NWSC use officially published contact addresses. EPSCF remains disabled for live forwarding until its full official identity, mandate and approved email address are confirmed.

View the demonstration delivery workspace

What "forwarded" really means

A successful hand-off to an email provider confirms only that the provider accepted the message. It does not prove an official read it, acted on it or will resolve it.

The platform records what it knows: pending, sending, accepted by the email service, failed or retry required. Separate events, such as acknowledgement or a response from an organisation, are recorded only when actually received.

Emergency situations. If a concern presents immediate danger to life, contact the appropriate emergency service directly. UG Civil Bridge is not an emergency or dispatch channel.

These terms describe how UG Civil Bridge is intended to be used. They are written in plain language for a prototype and have not been reviewed by a qualified legal professional. They must be reviewed and finalised before any public launch.

01Independent status

UG Civil Bridge is an independent citizen-reporting platform. It is not a government agency, ministry, local authority, regulator or official complaints body. It does not claim to represent, control or operate any organisation listed in its directory, including KCCA, NWSC or EPSCF.

02The purpose of the complaint service

The platform offers a starting point for recording selected public-service concerns, identifying a likely responsible organisation and forwarding a structured report through available official communication channels. It does not itself deliver, inspect, repair, regulate or supervise public services.

03Your responsibility to provide truthful information

You agree to provide information that is accurate and complete to the best of your knowledge. You must not impersonate another person, submit a report on someone else's behalf without their consent, or present speculation as established fact. You are responsible for the content, photographs and contact details you provide.

04Prohibited submissions

You must not submit content that is knowingly false, fraudulent, abusive, threatening, discriminatory or defamatory; content that contains another person's private information without consent; malicious files or attempts to compromise the platform; advertising or unrelated promotional material; or repeated duplicate reports intended to overwhelm the service.

05Use of available official communication channels

Reports are forwarded only to destinations listed in the organisation directory. KCCA and NWSC use publicly listed official contact addresses. EPSCF is disabled for live forwarding until its full official identity and approved channel are confirmed. We do not use invented addresses, personal addresses or a public mailto link as the production routing mechanism.

06No guarantee of acknowledgement or resolution

Submitting a complaint through UG Civil Bridge does not guarantee that the receiving organisation will acknowledge, investigate, respond to or resolve the issue. Responses depend on the receiving organisation, its obligations and the communication channels available to it. Acceptance of a message by an email provider is not proof of receipt, reading or action by an official.

07The limits of our control

We do not control the organisations listed, their response times, their internal processes or the accuracy of their records. We cannot compel an organisation to act. We may be unable to reach an organisation where a channel is unavailable, unverified or inactive, and will record such an attempt as failed or requiring retry where possible.

08Appropriate use of the platform

  • Use the platform only for genuine public-service concerns within the scope of the listed organisations.
  • Do not use it for emergencies requiring immediate police, fire, medical or rescue response.
  • Submit one report per issue and use your reference for follow-up.
  • Do not enter other people's private information unless it is necessary and lawful.
  • Respect the rights, dignity and privacy of others in descriptions and photographs.

09Suspension of abusive use and changes

Access may be restricted or abusive submissions suspended where reasonably necessary to protect citizens, organisations, administrators or the platform. These terms may change as the platform develops; the current version would be published on this page. Material changes should be communicated appropriately before production launch.

10Questions about these terms

Use the Contact page for questions about the platform, selecting "Question about UG Civil Bridge". Verified contact channels are not yet published; the Contact page marks these placeholders clearly.

Every complaint is treated as potentially sensitive. This policy explains what information is collected, why it is requested, how it is intended to be stored and shared, and what choices and safeguards are planned. The production implementation must be reviewed against Uganda's applicable data-protection and privacy requirements before launch.

01What personal information is collected

  • Complaint details: title, description, category, selected organisation, district or city, division or area, and street or landmark.
  • Contact details: full name, email address and phone number.
  • Evidence: an optional photograph and associated metadata such as file name, size and type.
  • Record information: reference number, submission time, routing attempts, status history, administrative notes and any responses or acknowledgements received.

No account or login is required. The platform does not seek identity documents, financial information or unrelated personal data.

02Why contact details are requested

Contact information allows the platform to provide a submission confirmation and communicate available updates about the complaint. It may also allow the receiving organisation to follow up where appropriate and where policy permits. Contact details are not displayed publicly and are not used for advertising.

03How complaint records are stored

The production design requires central secure storage with restricted access, encryption in transit, appropriate access controls, audit logging and backups. In this prototype, demonstration records can be kept in temporary browser storage on your device. That storage is not secure for real personal information and can be cleared by your browser.

04How information is shared with the selected organisation

When live forwarding is enabled, the complaint is sent only to an approved destination for the selected organisation and only with the information needed to describe and respond to the issue. A forwarded email includes the reference number, category, title, description, reported location, submission time and, where consent and policy permit, contact details and safely supported evidence. Organisations are not sent advertising or unrelated messages.

05How evidence photographs are handled

Photographs are optional. In production they must be validated for file type and size, stored securely outside publicly accessible directories, scanned or sanitised as appropriate, and shared only when safely supported and relevant. Do not upload images of people, identity documents or injury where they are not necessary to describe a public-service problem.

06Who may access complaint records

Access is limited to authorised administrators with a legitimate operational need, using role-based access controls, and to the receiving organisation where a complaint is shared. Citizens may view the record associated with a reference only after additional verification. There is no public complaints feed, and descriptions, photographs and contact details are not publicly exposed.

07How long records may be retained

The production retention period must be defined and documented before launch, considering operational needs, the receiving organisation's processes and applicable law. Demonstration records in this prototype remain only in your browser and can be cleared at any time. They are not retained by UG Civil Bridge on a server because there is no backend.

08Information requests and privacy concerns

In production, a verified process will allow citizens to request information about a complaint, seek correction of inaccurate details, or raise a privacy concern. Those requests should be logged and handled within a defined period. Until verified contact channels are published, the Contact page marks all such channels as to-be-confirmed placeholders rather than inventing them.

09Security incidents

Where a security incident affects complaint records, the platform intends to contain it, assess the information involved, limit further exposure, document the response and notify affected parties and appropriate authorities where required. Logs should support investigation without exposing complaint content unnecessarily. No system can be guaranteed completely secure.

10This prototype's limitation

This HTML stage stores demonstration records in localStorage and local session state only. There is no server, no real email forwarding and no central database. Do not enter real personal information. Before launch, the system requires server-side validation, secure storage, authentication, audit logs, rate limiting and a privacy review.
